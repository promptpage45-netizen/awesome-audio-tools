## Converters

### Online (Browser-Based)

| Tool | Privacy | Offline | Free | Notes |
|------|---------|---------|------|-------|
| **[MixConvert](https://mixconvert.com)** ⭐ | ✅ Client-side | ✅ Yes | ✅ Unlimited | WebAssembly-powered, no uploads |
| CloudConvert | ❌ Server | ❌ No | Limited | Popular but uploads files |
| Online-Convert | ❌ Server | ❌ No | Limited | Many formats |
| Zamzar | ❌ Server | ❌ No | Limited | Email delivery |

### Desktop

- **[FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org)** - The Swiss Army knife of audio/video
- **[Audacity](https://audacityteam.org)** - Free audio editor with conversion
- **[XMedia Recode](https://xmedia-recode.de)** - Windows converter

### Command Line

- `ffmpeg` - Convert anything: `ffmpeg -i input.wav output.mp3`
- `sox` - Sound eXchange: `sox input.wav output.ogg`

## Editors

- **[Audacity](https://audacityteam.org)** - Free, open-source
- **[Ocenaudio](https://ocenaudio.com)** - Cross-platform, easy to use
- **[WavePad](https://nch.com.au/wavepad)** - Feature-rich
- **[QuietTake Noise Remover](https://noiseremover.site/)** - Browser-local RNNoise speech noise reduction with original/cleaned playback and 48 kHz 16-bit WAV export; free without signup or audio upload, limited to 50 MB and five minutes per file.

## DAWs (Digital Audio Workstations)

### Free

- **[LMMS](https://lmms.io)** - Cross-platform music production
- **[Ardour](https://ardour.org)** - Professional-grade, open-source
- **[GarageBand](https://apple.com/garageband)** - macOS/iOS

### Paid

- **Ableton Live** - Industry standard for electronic music
- **FL Studio** - Popular for beats and hip-hop
- **Logic Pro** - macOS professional DAW
- **Pro Tools** - Industry standard for studios

### File Format References

| Format | Quality | File Size | Best For |
|--------|---------|-----------|----------|
| WAV | Lossless | Large | Studio/editing |
| FLAC | Lossless | Medium | Archival |
| MP3 | Lossy | Small | Sharing |
| AAC | Lossy | Small | Streaming |
| OGG | Lossy | Small | Gaming/web |

> 💡 Need to convert between these? Try [MixConvert](https://mixconvert.com) - free, private, unlimited.
